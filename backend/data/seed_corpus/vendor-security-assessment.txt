Third-Party Vendor Security Assessment - Brightline Payments Inc.

Assessment Date: April 2026
Conducted by: Vendor Risk Management, Information Security
Classification: Confidential

Scope

This assessment covers Brightline Payments Inc., engaged as a payment
processing subprocessor for the checkout service. The review covers
Brightline's SOC 2 Type II report (period ending December 2025), a
questionnaire response, and a follow-up technical interview with
Brightline's security team conducted on April 14, 2026.

Summary Rating

Brightline receives an overall rating of Low-Medium Risk, with one finding
rated Medium and no findings rated High or Critical. The vendor is approved
for continued use, contingent on remediation of the Medium finding within
90 days per section 6.

Data Handled

Brightline processes cardholder data (PAN, expiry, CVV at time of
transaction only, not retained) and transaction metadata including
purchase amount, merchant category code, and a tokenised customer
identifier. Brightline does not receive customer names, addresses, or
email addresses; these remain within our own systems and are matched to
the tokenised identifier only on our side.

Certifications and Compliance

Brightline holds a current PCI DSS Level 1 certification, the highest
tier, appropriate given transaction volume above 6 million per year. The
SOC 2 Type II report covers the Security and Availability trust service
criteria with no exceptions noted by the auditor. Brightline is not
currently certified against ISO 27001, which is noted as an observation
but not a finding, since PCI DSS Level 1 provides substantially overlapping
control coverage for the services in scope.

Findings

Finding 1 (Medium): Brightline's incident notification commitment in the
current contract is 72 hours from confirmed breach, whereas our vendor
security standard requires 24 hours for any vendor handling cardholder
data. Brightline's security team confirmed in the technical interview that
72 hours is their standard SLA but indicated willingness to negotiate a
24-hour commitment for our specific contract given our transaction volume.
Remediation: Legal to renegotiate the notification clause within 90 days
of this assessment; if not achieved, the account team must escalate to the
CISO for a risk-acceptance decision before contract renewal.

Finding 2 (Low): Brightline's questionnaire response indicated that
production database backups are encrypted with AES-256 but did not specify
whether backup encryption keys are rotated on a defined schedule. The
technical interview clarified that keys are rotated annually via a managed
KMS, which is acceptable, but this detail should be added to Brightline's
next questionnaire response to avoid re-raising this as an open question in
future assessments.

Encryption and Key Management

Data in transit uses TLS 1.2 or higher for all API connections; TLS 1.0 and
1.1 are disabled at the load balancer. Data at rest is encrypted with
AES-256 using a managed key service; Brightline does not have access to
raw unencrypted card data outside of the tokenisation service boundary,
which runs in a segmented network zone with restricted access logged and
reviewed monthly.

Access Control

Brightline enforces multi-factor authentication for all administrative
access to production systems and maintains a documented least-privilege
access model reviewed quarterly. Privileged access to the tokenisation
service specifically requires a second approver and is time-boxed to 8
hours per grant, consistent with our own vendor access standard.

Business Continuity

Brightline maintains an active-active architecture across two AWS regions
with an documented RTO of 15 minutes and RPO of 5 minutes. The most recent
disaster recovery test was conducted in February 2026 and met both
objectives; the test report was provided as part of this assessment and is
retained in the vendor risk system.

Recommendation

Continue use of Brightline as a payment processing subprocessor, subject to
tracking Finding 1 to closure within 90 days. Re-assessment is scheduled
for April 2027, or earlier if Brightline reports a material change to its
environment, a new subprocessor, or a security incident affecting our data.
