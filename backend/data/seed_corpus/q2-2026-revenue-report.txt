Meridian Analytics - Q2 2026 Revenue Report

Prepared by Finance & Strategy
Distribution: Board of Directors, Executive Leadership

Executive Summary

Meridian Analytics closed Q2 2026 with total revenue of $18.4 million,
representing 22% year-over-year growth and a 6% increase over Q1 2026. Net
revenue retention reached 128%, up from 119% in the prior quarter, driven
primarily by expansion within the enterprise segment. Gross margin held
steady at 74%, in line with the twelve-month trailing average.

Revenue by Segment

The Enterprise segment contributed $11.2 million, or 61% of total revenue,
growing 31% year-over-year. The Mid-Market segment contributed $5.1 million,
growing 14% year-over-year. The Self-Serve segment contributed $2.1 million,
essentially flat versus Q1, reflecting the planned sunset of the legacy
pricing tier announced in March.

Annual recurring revenue (ARR) exited the quarter at $71.6 million, up from
$65.9 million at the end of Q1. New ARR bookings totalled $8.9 million, of
which $6.2 million came from new logos and $2.7 million from expansion
within the existing customer base. Churned ARR was $2.3 million, yielding a
gross revenue retention rate of 94%.

Regional Performance

North America remained the largest region at 58% of total revenue, growing
19% year-over-year. EMEA grew 27% year-over-year to reach 29% of total
revenue, its fastest growth rate in six quarters, attributed to the London
and Berlin enterprise sales teams reaching full ramp. APAC grew 34%
year-over-year off a smaller base, now representing 13% of total revenue.

Customer Metrics

The company ended the quarter with 1,847 paying customers, up from 1,791 at
the end of Q1. The number of customers with an annual contract value above
$100,000 grew from 212 to 241. Average contract value across the enterprise
segment increased to $186,000, up from $171,000 in Q1, driven by increased
attach rates on the analytics add-on module, which now sits at 44% attach
among enterprise accounts, up from 37%.

Cost Structure and Profitability

Total operating expenses were $16.1 million, of which $7.4 million was
sales and marketing, $5.2 million was research and development, and $3.5
million was general and administrative. Operating margin improved to
negative 9%, compared to negative 15% in Q1, continuing the path toward the
board-approved target of profitability by Q4 2026. Free cash flow was
negative $1.1 million, an improvement of $2.4 million versus the prior
quarter.

Sales Efficiency

The magic number, calculated as net new ARR in the quarter divided by prior
quarter sales and marketing spend, was 0.83, up from 0.71 in Q1. Customer
acquisition cost payback period improved to 14 months from 17 months,
reflecting both improved close rates and a higher average contract value.

Outlook for Q3 2026

Management guided to Q3 2026 revenue of $19.6 million to $20.1 million,
implying year-over-year growth of 20% to 23%. The guidance assumes no
material change in the macroeconomic environment and reflects the planned
general availability launch of the forecasting module in early August,
which is expected to lift attach rates in the mid-market segment
specifically. The board has approved an incremental $1.5 million in sales
headcount investment for the second half of the year, concentrated in the
EMEA region given its outsized growth this quarter.

Risks and Considerations

The self-serve segment's flat growth bears monitoring; if the legacy tier
sunset does not convert as many accounts to the new pricing structure as
modelled, self-serve revenue could decline in Q3 rather than stabilise.
Foreign exchange movements, particularly a strengthening dollar against the
euro, reduced reported EMEA revenue by an estimated $340,000 relative to
constant currency. A prolonged strengthening of the dollar would continue to
create a modest headwind to reported international revenue in future
quarters.
